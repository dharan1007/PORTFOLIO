I am Poduvu Dharantej Reddy, an AI & Data Science engineering student and product builder. My work spans end-user products and the less visible infrastructure that makes agentic and data-intensive systems safer, more inspectable and more useful.

I tend to work from the system contract outward: what state is canonical, what can mutate, what evidence proves completion, what survives failure, and what the user actually receives when the system says it is done.

That is why the portfolio ranges from social products such as RÆDIUS and music orchestration such as DAISH to infrastructure such as SPOOL, PACT, FAULTLINE, KATA, Codebase OS and ARKHE.
