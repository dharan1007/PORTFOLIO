The portfolio uses the original certificate's dates and removes the unsupported three-month label. The role, contribution areas and mentor are sourced from the owner-supplied professional brief; the certificate independently supports the internship period.

The Great Learning document gives November 2023 without a day. No exact day is inferred. QR-code extraction did not yield a credential-specific verification URL; the generic Infosys verification portal is not presented as a specific credential-verification link. Oracle’s small footer identifier is omitted because it was not transcribed with sufficient confidence. No grades, expiration dates, accreditations or course modules were invented.

Each dataset record includes its source Drive ID, original filename, SHA-256 hash and extraction/inspection note. `node scripts/verify-certifications.mjs` verifies the local original hashes and preview presence.
